# Warehouse Data Analysis Project

## 📊 Overview
This project analyzes **10 warehouses** located across three U.S. states (Kentucky, Michigan, and Tennessee). It was developed as part of the Google Data Analytics coursework to practice data cleaning, aggregation, PivotTable summarization, and business data interpretation.

## 🔍 Business Question
*How do warehouse capacity and staffing levels compare across states, individual warehouses, and capacity ranges?*

## 📁 Dataset Contents
* **Warehouse_table**: Cleaned source data including warehouse ID, alias, maximum capacity, total employees, and state.
* **Statistics by State**: Summary of total capacity, total employees, and warehouse count per state.
* **By Capacity Range**: Grouping of warehouses based on their maximum capacity ranges.

## 📌 Key Findings
* **Tennessee** holds the highest total capacity (1,990) and employee count (57) across 4 warehouses.
* **Michigan** closely follows with 1,810 total capacity and 55 employees across 3 warehouses.
* **Kentucky** has a lower total capacity (725) and employee count (32) compared to the other regions.
* The most common warehouse capacity range is **200–399**.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, PivotTables, Summary Statistics)
